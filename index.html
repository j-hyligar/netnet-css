Welcome to My Page!

I themed this template page off of my love for binding books and print found in my bookmarks. Project Gutenberg takes public domain texts and makes them available for print, bookbinders like myself often print these and bind them to test techniques, skills, or just make something nice. The Funday Press is a newspaper with only fun and games designed by Katrina Romulo. She has a fun YouTube channel. Heyzine hosts free services to turn PDFs into different styles of flipbooks and share them. I use them to demo typeset pieces Ive designed before printing. Bookbinder imposes PDFs so when they are bound they line up perfectly.
